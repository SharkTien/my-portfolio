# Portfolio site (home, project overviews, chat entry)

Scope: the whole portfolio site, home route plus `/project/:slug` overviews; `/chat` keeps its function and inherits palette tokens. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), serving AI hiring teams and freelance web clients equally.

Job: each audience finds its door (AI work / client websites) in the first viewport, sees proof, and reaches the CV chatbot, CV download or contact.

Constraints: keep the CV chatbot, project overview pages with cover morph, light/dark switch; keep all content; avatar.jpg is the portrait; avoid template look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an issued, security-printed credential; every claim carries a serial number and a seal that links to its proof. Refuses the split hero plus card grid every developer portfolio ships.

OWN-WORLD: Security paper in pale celadon owns every region, engraved guilloche rosettes and microtext rules carry structure, ink teal for type and line work, vermilion seal as the only active/verified/hover mark, gold foil only on the photo hologram. Dark mode is the same document under a UV lamp: violet-black ground, fluorescent fibres and lettering. Type: expanded grotesk caps for fields and display, a narrow mono for serials, dates and the MRZ.

STORY: The visitor reads a credential data page, sees who Felik is and what he ships, chooses a door, checks the stamped evidence (awards, certificates, papers), then contacts or interrogates the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width data page. Left third: avatar.jpg in a photo field under a cursor-tracked holographic foil, guilloche rosette behind. Right: field grid (Surname/Given names, Known as FELIK, Occupation, Based in, Issued by UIT), two large doors "AI Engineering" and "Client Websites", primary stamped action "Chat with my CV", secondary "Download CV". MRZ strip in mono runs the full bottom edge.

FORM: grounded candidate 7 of 7 (Issued Credential), seed:0df947911add2fbb. Signature interaction: holographic foil sheen tracking pointer over photo and seals; seals stamp down when evidence scrolls in; UV toggle re-lights the document. Motion grammar: stamp (scale-down + ink spread), foil sheen, register lines ruling in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
